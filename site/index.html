The community behind the archive

About root

root is an independent, student-led engineering and IT community in Jordan. We bring curious people together to learn by doing, build real projects, share knowledge, and explore things beyond the classroom.

Through collaborative projects, workshops, experimentation, and competitions, we make room to try things out and learn from each other.

A little less starting from scratch.

root/archive is our first public project: a shared collection of course materials, initially covering Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Cybersecurity at AUM. It brings students’ learning resources together so the next person has somewhere to start.

Anyone can browse and download. Contributions currently require an eligible student account, and every submission is reviewed before it appears in the archive.

Built by a community.

Practice questions, summaries, notes, test banks, and other resources can make a difference. If you have something you’re allowed to share, help the collection grow.

root and root/archive are independent community projects, not official university services.

# root/archive Terms of Use

## The service

Omar Khaldoon Jaber Tahrawi operates root/archive as an individual through root, an independent, unincorporated student-led technology community based in Jordan. The archive organizes student learning resources, initially for courses at the American University of Madaba (AUM). These terms cover root/archive. The root community website at rootamman.com is a separate service. See the [Independence and Non-Affiliation Statement](root-archive-independence-statement.md).

## Browsing and downloads

Anyone can browse and download approved resources without signing in. You may download and use resources for your own study. You need authorization for uses such as reposting, redistributing or reselling a resource unless the stated licence for that material, the rights holder or applicable law permits the use. Follow any licence and attribution requirements shown for the resource. These terms preserve permissions you already have under an applicable licence or law.

Use the archive without disrupting the service, trying to access private files or accounts, or bypassing submission and access controls. Use study resources consistently with the academic rules that apply to your course and assessments.

## Contributions and moderation

Submissions require sign-in with an eligible student account, currently an eligible AUM student Google account. Submissions go through private moderation. The [Contributor Declaration](root-archive-contributor-declaration.md) describes the permission you give for each submission. Contributors retain ownership of their original work and can choose whether to receive public credit.

We may reject, correct, replace, restrict or remove material under the [Content and Copyright Policy](root-archive-content-and-copyright-policy.md) and the [Takedown and Dispute Procedure](root-archive-takedown-and-dispute-procedure.md). Approval applies to the version reviewed. The archive may change or stop offering a resource.

## Use your course’s current instructions

Resources are study aids. They may contain errors or cover an older syllabus. Check the current requirements with your course or instructor before relying on them for assessed work. These terms preserve any mandatory rights or remedies available under applicable law.

See the [Privacy Notice](root-archive-privacy-notice.md) for information about personal data. Contact [privacy@rootamman.com](mailto:privacy@rootamman.com) or, as a backup, [omar@rootamman.com](mailto:omar@rootamman.com).

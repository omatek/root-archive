# root/archive Takedown and Dispute Procedure

## Send a report

Email [privacy@rootamman.com](mailto:privacy@rootamman.com). If you cannot reach us there, use [omar@rootamman.com](mailto:omar@rootamman.com). Anyone can report a concern. If you are a contributor requesting withdrawal, say so and identify your submission or resource.

Include the resource link, ID or title, the part at issue, the reason for your request and a way to contact you. For a copyright claim, identify the original work and explain which rights you hold or whose authority you have to act. If the concern involves exposed personal information, a live assessment or another urgent risk, say so. Describe sensitive evidence briefly and avoid sending more than we need to locate and understand the problem.

## How we handle reports

1. **Review and contain.** We check what the report identifies and may ask for specific details or proof of authority. We prioritize credible reports of exposed personal information, live assessment leaks or serious infringement and may suspend access while we review.
2. **Check the facts.** We consider the applicable permissions, course or confidentiality rules, and the exact version at issue. When appropriate, we give the contributor enough information to respond. We share identifying information only when necessary and may limit contact where an official process or someone’s safety requires it.
3. **Decide and respond.** We may leave the resource up, correct or replace it, keep it restricted, or remove it. We explain the outcome to the people directly involved when we can. Either party can ask us to reconsider with a specific correction or new information. We restore access only when the concern is resolved for the version being restored.

When we apply removal or withdrawal to a resource, we immediately revoke public access through the archive and handle private files according to the [Privacy Notice](root-archive-privacy-notice.md). Email reports require action by the operator; receipt alone does not automatically revoke access. Copies that people already downloaded or that others indexed may remain outside our control. We keep only the evidence needed for the case, contributor permission and moderation records under the approved retention schedule, or a specific legal obligation.

For a personal-data request or privacy complaint, see the [Privacy Notice](root-archive-privacy-notice.md). Privacy rights and complaints have their own legal deadlines. We handle formal orders according to their verified scope and deadlines.

# root/archive Content and Copyright Policy

## What you can submit

We accept original student-authored notes, summaries, explanations and original practice questions, including collections of independently written practice questions. Write in your own words. Where material has co-authors, obtain authority from everyone whose permission is needed before you upload it.

Past examination papers are excluded. Keep copied or confidential exam questions, answer keys for restricted assessments, leaked assessment material and confidential course content out of submissions. This applies even when the material is old or already circulating.

The initial catalog focuses on original student work. If your work includes an excerpt, image, diagram or other material someone else owns, identify it and show the permission or licence that covers the proposed use. Copied university teaching materials and third-party copyrighted works require rights clearance before we can consider publishing them. A permission to copy also needs to be consistent with applicable course rules and confidentiality restrictions. A citation, access to a copy or an educational purpose alone is insufficient.

Remove names, student numbers, grades, email addresses and other identifiable personal information about others before uploading. Check comments, revision history and file properties as well as the visible pages. Material with identifiable personal information requires separate lawful clearance before we can consider releasing it. Optional credit for your own contribution is handled separately.

## What moderation covers

We review the specific version for originality, permissions, privacy and academic restrictions. We may ask for a clean revision or evidence that you have authority to share the material. We may decline a file if we cannot resolve a material concern. Submitting a file gives us the limited permission described in the [Contributor Declaration](root-archive-contributor-declaration.md).

You keep ownership of your original work. You can choose public credit under your name or a pseudonym, or leave the credit field empty. Where existing rights require attribution or a licence notice, we keep that information with the resource or decline to host it. We keep your private sign-in identity separate from public credit.

The archive permits downloads for personal study. Further reuse follows the [Terms of Use](root-archive-terms-of-use.md) and any licence that applies to the particular material.

## Reporting or withdrawing material

Follow the [Takedown and Dispute Procedure](root-archive-takedown-and-dispute-procedure.md) to report a resource or withdraw permission for your own contribution. Contact [privacy@rootamman.com](mailto:privacy@rootamman.com), with [omar@rootamman.com](mailto:omar@rootamman.com) as a backup.

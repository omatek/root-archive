## What we collect and why

| When processing starts | Information and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| When you open a page or download a resource | We use technical request data, such as IP address, request time, page or file URL, browser details and security signals, to deliver the service and prevent abuse. Browsing and downloading require no account. |
| When you use student sign-in or the upload security check | Google provides an identity credential containing a verified student email, an account identifier and account-domain details. We use it to check eligibility. Cloudflare Turnstile uses browser and network signals to check for automated abuse. Google sign-in and Cloudflare security and administrator sign-in can use cookies or similar browser mechanisms. |
| When you submit a resource | We associate your email and account identifier with the file, original filename, catalog information, and any optional credit. We record the accepted declaration and contributor privacy consent separately in a private receipt associated with your verified identity, submission event, original revision, file hash and server timestamp. Authorized moderators use these to review the material, manage revisions, record decisions and handle withdrawal or rights questions. We do not retain the raw Google credential as a permission record. Uploaded files can contain hidden information, such as author names, comments or revision history. |
| When a resource is published | The approved file and its catalog details become public. If you choose public credit, we display the name or pseudonym you provide. We do not automatically include your sign-in email or account identifier in the public catalog or download response. Identifying information embedded in the approved file can become public. People may save files or copy or index public information. |
| When you contact us | We use your contact details, correspondence and any necessary supporting information to respond to a request, report or dispute or to meet a legal duty. |
| When a moderator signs in or acts on a submission | We use administrator identity, session information and a record of decisions, reasons and timestamps to control access and account for changes. |

The archive does not build advertising profiles or rank contributors by behaviour. Eligibility and abuse checks are automated, including Google account checks and Cloudflare Turnstile security signals; publication decisions are made by a moderator. These statements describe the archive application, not every independent use of information by its providers.

We use the information needed to provide and secure the services you request under our [Terms of Use](root-archive-terms-of-use.md), including delivery, eligibility checks and restricted moderator access. We rely on your affirmative consent for the processing and transfer described below and on your separate choice to display public credit. We process information as necessary to fulfil privacy rights, comply with legal obligations or respond to valid orders under Jordanian law.

## Who can receive the information

Access to private submissions and case records is limited to people authorized to handle them.

- Cloudflare provides site hosting and request delivery through Pages and Workers, database storage through D1, file storage through R2, abuse checks through Turnstile and administrator access controls through Access. Its [Data Processing Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/) sets the safeguards for processing on our behalf. Cloudflare processes security signals to protect the archive and uses them to improve its bot detection, as described in the [Turnstile Privacy Notice](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/).
- Google Identity Services supplies the credential we use to check student eligibility. Google handles its own account service under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Our use of Google sign-in is subject to [Google’s OAuth policies](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/policies).
- We use Lark Mail to handle organizational correspondence, including privacy requests and content reports. Our Lark organization’s configured data residency is Japan. Lark handles customer mail under its [Customer Terms of Service](https://www.larksuite.com/en_us/customer-terms-of-service) and [Data Processing Addendum](https://www.larksuite.com/en_us/legal/data-processing-addendum).

We may share limited information when needed to resolve a claim or meet a legal obligation. A contributor may receive the parts of a report needed to respond. We limit identifying details to what the situation requires.

These services involve processing outside Jordan. Cloudflare hosts archive records and files outside Jordan, and its upload security check uses its global service. Google operates its account service internationally. The processor terms above provide security, rights assistance and deletion commitments for the customer data they cover. We restrict access to review files and receipts to authorized moderators and use encrypted storage and connections.

## How long we keep information

| Information | Approved retention requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Pending submissions | Up to 30 days without a moderation decision. |
| Rejected or expired uploaded files | Deleted within 7 days of rejection or expiry. |
| Published resources | Kept while published and authorized. |
| Removed or withdrawn resources | Public access through the archive is revoked immediately when the resource is removed or withdrawn. Private files are deleted within 7 days of removal or withdrawal. |
| Contributor permission and privacy receipts | Kept during review, while the resource is published and for up to 24 months after rejection or removal. |
| Moderation audit records | 24 months after the final moderation action. |
| Complaints and disputes | 12 months after resolution. |
| Application and security logs | Up to 30 days where we can configure retention. |
| Backups and recovery | On our Cloudflare Workers Free plan, D1 Time Travel retains database history for 7 days. Lark Mail can keep a recoverable copy after a message is deleted, subject to its mail recovery settings. Provider recovery copies can outlast active records. |

The operator applies these deadlines through moderation, cleanup and periodic record deletion. The application does not automatically expire pending submissions, withdrawn files, permission records or moderation history.

An unresolved dispute, legal hold or mandatory retention can require a limited exception. We keep only what is needed for that purpose, restrict access and end the exception when the justification ends. We keep necessary evidence separately from files scheduled for deletion. We can stop future downloads through the archive. Copies already saved elsewhere may remain available.

## Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to appear in the public credit field and ask us to change or remove your credit. Subject to Jordanian law, you can ask about our processing, access or obtain a copy of your data, correct or update it, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent where it is the basis, or request erasure or anonymization. You can also request portability where applicable and receive breach notice where the law requires it.

Email us with the request and any relevant resource or submission reference. You can withdraw your contributor privacy consent through the same addresses. We stop processing that relies on the consent when we receive a verified withdrawal and keep limited records where there is a separate legal basis to do so. We may ask for the minimum information needed to verify your identity or authority and can help if you need an accessible way to make the request. Please keep passwords and sign-in credentials out of your message. We handle rights requests and privacy complaints under the applicable Jordanian deadlines. If your privacy complaint remains unresolved after you raise it with us, you can use the [Ministry’s personal data protection complaint service](https://modee.gov.jo/AR/Pages/الخدمات).
